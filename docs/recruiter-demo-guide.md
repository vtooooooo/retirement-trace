# Five-minute recruiter demo

Sign in with your assigned demonstration account. Open **Demo walkthrough** in the sidebar. Start at the priority queue, then select **Obligation without payment record** to see why the ledger evidence warrants review. Switch to the identifier conflict case and compare source records and model fields. Follow a conflicted relationship in the evidence graph and open its source record. Compare the recommended action with alternatives, then inspect the case timeline, held-out benchmarks, and audit export.

Browse the screenshots in [screenshots](screenshots/). These captures show the original workspace layout. The hosted version loads persistent synthetic records from the API. Decision buttons depend on the account role and current case state.
