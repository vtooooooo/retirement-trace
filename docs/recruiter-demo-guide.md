# Five-minute recruiter demo

Open **Demo walkthrough** in the sidebar. Start at the priority queue, then select **Obligation without payment record** to see why the ledger evidence warrants review. Switch to the identifier conflict case and compare source records and model fields. Follow a conflicted relationship in the evidence graph and open its source record. Compare the recommended action with alternatives, then inspect the case timeline, held-out benchmarks, and audit export.

Browse the screenshots in [screenshots](screenshots/). The public workflows use persisted synthetic data exported at build time. Decision recording is disabled in this mode.
